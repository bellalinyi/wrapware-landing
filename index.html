How it works

Three steps, no tools, no tape.

The core never changes — only what's wrapped around it. Every wrap in the library fits every tumbler in the lineup.

01 / PICK A CORE

Choose your tumbler

20oz or 30oz double-wall steel, in Marigold, Fern, or bare Steel. The core is what you keep for years.

02 / CHOOSE A WRAP

Pick from the library

Botanicals, harvest prints, solids, and small-batch drops. Each wrap is water-resistant canvas over a flexible magnet sheet.

03 / SNAP IT ON

Line up and press

Six hidden points pull the wrap flush against the core. No ridges, no gaps — just a satisfying click into place.

The hardware

What's actually holding it on.

Dishwasher-safe core

Wraps come off before a wash — the bare steel core takes the top rack just fine.

Water-resistant canvas

Coated cotton duck face, so condensation and spills wipe off before they soak in.
